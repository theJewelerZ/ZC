# Product Brief

## North Star

> Every feature must either help a customer make a confident hiring decision or
> help Zarka Construction deliver a better project. If it does neither, it does
> not belong.

> Every completed project should make the next customer more confident in hiring
> Zarka Construction.

## Business mission

Zarka Construction exists to become the premier Golf Simulator Construction
Specialist. The public website establishes trust and creates qualified
conversations. The private operating system supports the work from consultation
through project delivery, documentation, completion, proof, and future referral.

Software is infrastructure for a better construction business. Zarka is not
building software merely because it is interesting.

## Strategic position

**Category:** Golf Simulator Construction Specialist

**Positioning sentence:** Zarka Construction evaluates, plans, prepares, and
completes defined specialty-construction scopes for premium golf simulator
environments in homes and commercial golf settings.

**Protected philosophy:** We don't build golf simulators. We build the spaces
where great golf happens.

**Hero idea:** We Build the Room Around the Game.

**Brand statement:** Built with precision. Delivered with integrity.

Zarka is not presented as an equipment dealer, architect, engineer, permit
authority, licensed general contractor for an entire facility, or party
responsible for work outside an agreed written scope.

## Business lifecycle

The operating system is organized around:

```text
Lead → Consultation → Room Review → Project → Inside the Build
→ Completed Project → Portfolio → Future Referral
```

Each completed project should leave behind organized operational knowledge,
authentic photography, a factual completion record, and approved public proof.

## Current audiences

| Audience | Need | Zarka response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homeowner | Confidence that the desired room and experience are being considered carefully | Explain evaluation, planning, specialty scope, and the consultation path |
| Golf facility owner | A specialist for the simulator environment inside a commercial setting | Show relevant capabilities without claiming complete facility construction |
| Licensed general contractor | Clear specialty boundaries and coordination responsibilities | Define Zarka's work and identify responsibilities assigned to qualified trades |
| Equipment provider | A prepared environment that supports known equipment requirements | Position Zarka as the environment specialist, not a competing dealer |
| Customer following a project | Authentic evidence of how Zarka works | Publish founder-approved Inside the Build updates without exposing operations |

## Present public scope

Subject to room review and a written project scope, work may include:

- room evaluation;
- simulator and construction planning;
- simulator-room preparation and framing;
- impact-screen structures and custom layered screens;
- curtains and enclosure details;
- wall and ceiling protection;
- turf, stance areas, and hitting surfaces;
- finish carpentry, trim, transitions, and finish detailing;
- coordination with equipment providers or qualified trades where required.

These are possible scope elements, not a promise that every project includes
every element.

## Current operating capabilities

- Production simulator-specialist website.
- Durable consultation records.
- Optional private consultation photographs.
- Server-side validation, Turnstile, rate limiting, and Resend notifications.
- Founder-only consultation list/detail, status, notes, and signed photo access.
- Supabase email/password authentication with a server-side founder allowlist.

Projects, Inside the Build, Field Mode, site controls, a populated portfolio, and
later integrations remain planned. The canonical sequence is in

## Customer and founder value

Customers should be able to:

- understand what Zarka does and does not do;
- understand why every space requires evaluation;
- request an on-site consultation or guided remote room review;
- see authentic work and documented progress as approved projects accumulate;
- make a more confident hiring decision from proof rather than claims.

The founder should be able to:

- receive and review qualified consultation information durably;
- organize projects and field documentation;
- publish selected proof without exposing private operations;
- derive case studies, portfolio content, education, and marketing from the
  project source;
- reduce duplicate work only after a process is stable enough to automate.

## Conversion actions

- **Primary:** Request a Simulator Consultation.
- **Secondary:** See What We Build / See How the Space Is Evaluated.
- **Review choice:** On-site consultation or guided remote room review.

A request begins a conversation. It is not an equipment quote, feasibility
guarantee, design engagement, permit commitment, or construction agreement.

## Differentiators

- Focus on the room and experience rather than an equipment catalog.
- Practical construction experience applied to a specialized environment.
- Planning, impact structures, protection, surfaces, carpentry, curtains, and
  finish integration considered together.
- Organized scopes, assumptions, communication, and project documentation.
- Authentic project evidence captured during the work.
- Honest identification of responsibilities belonging to equipment providers or
  qualified trades.

## Product principles

- The website sells trust.
- Construction supports the golf experience.
- Consultation comes before quotation.
- Private operations stay separate from public publishing.
- Authentic founder-owned photography outranks stock or generated imagery.
- Proof outranks promises.
- Document first. Automate second.
- Stay simple until complexity creates measurable value.
- AI may draft. Founder approves. Always.

## Non-goals and identity boundaries

Zarka is not becoming:

- an equipment store or dealer directory;
- a generic CRM or SaaS platform;
- a scheduling platform;
- a contractor marketplace or social network;
- a self-service estimator, configurator, or shopping cart;
- an unrestricted customer-account system;
- a source of stock, AI-generated, staged, or fictional project proof;
- an autonomous AI authority for scopes, prices, contracts, or commitments.

Do not publish unsupported licensing, insurance, geography, capacity, warranty,
manufacturer, schedule, price, review, award, customer, or project claims.

## Success model

Product success is measured by business evidence:

- qualified consultations;
- projects documented consistently;
- approved photography retained and usable;
- customers following authentic project progress;
- completed projects becoming case studies and portfolio proof;
- proof helping future customers decide;
- founder time saved without lowering quality or trust;
- no private information exposed through public publishing.

A feature or phase is not successful merely because it shipped.

## Measurable signals

Use PII-free measurements where practical:

- consultation completion and qualified-fit rate;
- consultation-to-project handoff once Phase 7 exists;
- documentation completeness by active project;
- number of approved project images and updates;
- Inside the Build engagement without identifying customers;
- consultations that reference project proof;
- completed-project-to-case-study/portfolio rate;
- repeat inquiries and referrals;
- founder adoption of field and dashboard workflows;
- failed upload, notification, publication, and recovery events.

Later numeric targets are set from measured baselines at each phase kickoff.

## Truth and privacy constraints

- Exact addresses, customer contact details, contracts, costs, private schedules,
  internal notes, and unapproved identities remain private.
- A public project requires construction to have begun, recorded rights/consent,
  founder activation, and individual update approval.
- Public content is derived from the private project source; it does not expose
  operational records directly.
- AI, analytics, logs, support messages, and public issue trackers must not
  receive unapproved customer or project information.
