# Premium Experience System

## Definition

Premium means calm, precise, restrained, architectural, confident, legible, crafted, consistent, and fast. It does not mean ornamental luxury, decorative charts, heavy motion, glass effects, or dense generic dashboard chrome.

## Shared principles

- Use navy and neutral surfaces for structure; reserve orange for primary action and priority.
- Create hierarchy with spacing, typography, alignment, and borders before adding shadow.
- Prefer one clear primary action per context.
- Make status understandable through words and structure, not color alone.
- Keep public storytelling editorial and founder operations direct.
- Preserve authentic project photography and approved brand assets.

## Public surfaces

Public pages prioritize confident headlines, measured line lengths, real imagery, quiet metadata, and clear consultation paths. Inside the Build reads as a curated journal. Empty states disclose that work is published intentionally without looking unfinished.

## Private surfaces

Founder pages use a consistent navigation, page heading, section introduction, status treatment, form rhythm, and mobile action pattern. Mission Control is action-first. Field Mode stays visually lean because speed and outdoor legibility outrank richness.

## Interaction

Touch targets are at least 44 pixels. Focus is strongly visible. Motion is limited to short color, border, and position transitions and is disabled when reduced motion is requested.

## Review standard

Every refinement must improve comprehension, confidence, task speed, accessibility, or consistency. Novelty alone is not a reason to change a layout.
